## Why
The 2025 epic described how to request, configure and launch a vision rider, but left eligibility boundaries, effective-date rules, launch evidence and cancellation handling unspecified. This change makes those decisions explicit before implementation so teams share a clear definition of coverage and readiness.

## What Changes
- Define which groups, plans, employees and dependents can receive the rider.
- Specify request deadlines and effective-date rules for enrollment, changes and termination.
- Identify decision owners and notification responsibilities.
- Define the evidence required to approve launch, including benefit testing and eligibility agreement with Fabrikam.
- Specify when launch must be blocked and how an active rider can be suspended or cancelled.
