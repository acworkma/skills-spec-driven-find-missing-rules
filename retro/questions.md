# Questions about the vision rider epic

Read `retro/original-epic.md`, then write the questions you would ask the product owner before anyone builds. Write
one question per numbered line, ending with a question mark. Write at least four of your own before you ask
Copilot for more.

1. Who reviews the configuration, and who can approve it?
2. Who decides which employer groups and medical plans may offer the vision rider?
3. Who resolves conflicts between the benefit grid, sales commitments and Fabrikam's coverage rules?
4. By when must requests be approved for July 1 coverage, and what happens to late requests?
5. When do enrollments, changes and terminations take effect, including retroactive changes?
6. Are dependents eligible, and whose coverage changes when an employee adds or drops the rider?
7. Which teams and external parties must be informed before a group's coverage starts or changes?
8. What evidence must UAT provide for sign-off, including contact lens claims and denied claims?
9. What proves that each group's configured benefits and Fabrikam's eligibility records agree before coverage begins?
10. What conditions must block a group's go-live, and who may authorize an exception?
11. Who can suspend or cancel an active rider, and what happens to affected members and claims already incurred?
